Privacy policy

Effective 27 September 2026. Covers the ShopVoice service at {{BASE_URL}}: the MCP connector, the sign-in and account pages, and the ShopVoice plugin, which only talks to this service.

Who we are

ShopVoice is part of GroceryClaw, an open-source project (MIT licence, github.com/vansyson1308/groceryclaw) run by an individual developer. Contact: {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

What we collect

  • Account data: your email address, a scrypt hash of your password (never the password itself), your language, and timestamps of sign-ins and failed sign-ins.
  • Shop data: products, stock levels, sales totals, suppliers, supplier invoices and purchase-order drafts. For a new account this is generated sample data. For a real shop it is the data you or your shop enter in GroceryClaw, or sync from your point-of-sale system if you turn that on.
  • Tool-call audit log: for each ShopVoice tool call, the tool name, its arguments with secrets removed, a short result summary, whether it succeeded, and how long it took.
  • Connection records: the apps you connected (for example Claude), which permissions you granted, and hashed tokens with their creation, use and expiry times.
  • Technical logs: IP address, time, request path and status, used for rate limiting, security and debugging.

What we do not collect: ShopVoice only receives what an app sends in a tool call, such as a product name or a date range. It never receives or stores your conversations with Claude, Claude's memory, chat history or your files. We do not collect payment details or health data.

Why we use it

  • To answer your requests about your shop and to record the reorders you confirm.
  • To keep each shop's data separate. Every query runs under Postgres row-level security for that one shop.
  • To secure the service: sign-in protection, rate limits and abuse detection.
  • To fix problems, using the audit log and technical logs.

We do not sell your data. We do not use it for advertising. We do not use it to train AI models.

Where it is stored and how it is protected

ShopVoice runs on Amazon Web Services in the United States (region us-east-1). Data travels over HTTPS (TLS). Disks are encrypted at rest. Tokens, authorization codes and client secrets are stored only as SHA-256 hashes. Passwords are stored only as scrypt hashes. The application's database role cannot read the sign-in tables directly.

How long we keep it

  • Tool-call audit log: 90 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Technical server logs: 14 days.
  • Expired or revoked tokens: deleted about 7 days after they expire. Unused app registrations: deleted after 30 days.
  • Account and shop data: until you delete your account. Demo-shop sample data is regenerated every day.

Your choices and rights

  • Disconnect an app at any time on the account page, or from Claude's connector settings.
  • Delete your account yourself on the account page. This takes effect immediately and erases your demo shop. You can also email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}; we complete requests within 30 days.
  • Access or correct your data: email us and we will reply within 30 days.

Who else processes data

  • Amazon Web Services hosts the service.
  • The app you connect (for example Anthropic's Claude, or Amazon Alexa) receives the tool results it asked for and processes them under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • KiotViet (a point-of-sale system) receives or sends data only if your shop turns on that optional sync in GroceryClaw.

We share nothing with anyone else unless the law requires it.

Children

ShopVoice is a business tool and is not intended for anyone under 18.
