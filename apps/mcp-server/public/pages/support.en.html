Support

Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. We reply within 2 business days, usually sooner.

What to include

  • Where you used ShopVoice Pay: the console, or an app such as Claude (web, desktop, mobile or Claude Code).
  • Roughly when it happened: date, time and timezone.
  • What you asked, and the exact message you saw. For Claude connection errors, include the reference ID that starts with ofid_ if one is shown.
  • If you have an account, its email address. Never send a password, a PayPal sign-in or a token.

Quick fixes

  • "The demo is busy" or "Too many new demo shops": wait a few minutes and try again. Each visitor gets a private sample shop, and new shops are rate limited.
  • Start over: use Reset demo at the top of the console. It erases your demo payments and history.
  • An order "needs your OK": it is over your spending rules. Approve or decline it in the console's Approvals tab, by saying "yes" or "no", or on the PayPal page. Nothing is held or charged until you approve.
  • The PayPal page asks you to sign in: it is PayPal's sandbox. Use the sandbox buyer account given in the project README, never your real PayPal account.
  • "Couldn't connect" in Claude: remove ShopVoice Pay under Customize → Connectors, add it again, and sign in in the popup. Check that your browser allows popups for claude.ai.
  • Payments say the connection is read-only: you unticked write access when connecting. Reconnect and leave it ticked.
  • Locked out after wrong passwords: wait 15 minutes and try again.
