Support

Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. We reply within 2 business days (Vietnam time, UTC+7), usually sooner.

What to include

  • The email address of your ShopVoice account. Never send your password.
  • Where you used ShopVoice (claude.ai, the Claude desktop or mobile app, Cowork, Claude Code, or Alexa) and roughly when (date, time and timezone).
  • What you asked and the exact error message. For Claude connection errors, include the reference ID that starts with ofid_ if one is shown.

Quick fixes

  • "Couldn't connect" or "Authorization failed" in Claude: remove ShopVoice under Customize → Connectors, add it again, and sign in in the popup. Check that your browser allows popups for claude.ai.
  • Reorders say the connection is read-only: you unticked "create and confirm purchase-order drafts" when connecting. Reconnect and leave it ticked.
  • Wrong shop: the account page shows which shop your account uses. Link a real shop there with an invite code.
  • Locked out after wrong passwords: wait 15 minutes and try again.
  • Stop access for an app: revoke it on the account page.

Security reports

Please report security issues privately to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} with "SECURITY" in the subject. We acknowledge reports within 2 business days.
