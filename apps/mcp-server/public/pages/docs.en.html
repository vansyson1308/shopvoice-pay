ShopVoice Pay documentation

ShopVoice Pay is a voice-first purchasing agent for independent grocers. Ask what is running low, reorder by voice, and it pays suppliers through PayPal, only within the spending rules you set. Money is held until delivery and charged only for what arrived. Use it by voice in the ShopVoice Pay console, or from Claude (web, desktop, mobile and Claude Code) through this MCP server.

Server URL: {{MCP_URL}}
Transport: Streamable HTTP (MCP protocol 2025-11-25). Sign-in: OAuth 2.1, a free ShopVoice Pay account.

Who it is for

ShopVoice Pay is for owners of independent grocery and convenience stores who reorder from a handful of regular suppliers and want that to take less of their day, without giving an AI their wallet. Every tool reads or writes only your own shop's records, and every payment goes through ShopVoice Pay's rules engine and your approvals.

Connect in Claude

From the directory

Open Customize → Connectors, find ShopVoice Pay and select Connect. A ShopVoice Pay sign-in window opens.

As a custom connector

  1. In Claude, open Customize → Connectors and select Add custom connector.
  2. Name: ShopVoice Pay. URL: {{MCP_URL}}. Leave the advanced OAuth fields empty.
  3. Select Connect. In the ShopVoice Pay window, sign in or create an account, check what the app may do, and select Allow.
  4. In a chat, turn ShopVoice Pay on from + → Connectors and ask, for example, "What's running low in my shop?"

Claude Code

claude mcp add --transport http shopvoice {{MCP_URL}}

Then run /mcp in a session and choose Authenticate to sign in.

The console

The ShopVoice Pay console is the voice app. Select Try the demo for your own private sample shop, then hold the button (or press Space) and speak, or type. It shows every tool call, your approvals, the payment ledger, your spending rules and your suppliers.

Your account and your shop

  • New accounts get a demo shop with sample data, labelled "Your demo shop (sample data)": a fictional US corner market with 47 products, 4 fictional suppliers, 30 days of sales, 3 supplier invoices, spending rules and a simulated PayPal account, so you can try every tool right away. The sample data resets once a day.
  • Real shops can be linked with an invite code from the shop's owner, on your account page. This hackathon demo does not offer real shops.
  • Prices are in US dollars. Payments run in PayPal's sandbox or a simulated PayPal: no real money moves in the demo.

How payments stay safe

  1. create_reorder_draft only writes drafts. It returns a one-time confirmation token that expires after 5 minutes and works only for your shop.
  2. confirm_reorder is marked destructive, so Claude asks before it runs. The AI can only propose: ShopVoice's own rules engine decides each payment, and only server code talks to PayPal.
  3. Within your rules, the money is held on PayPal, not charged. You are charged only for what is delivered; the rest is released.
  4. Over your rules (for example above your auto-pay limit, or a price jump), ShopVoice asks you directly: in Claude's own confirmation dialog when the app supports it, otherwise on PayPal's approval page. The approval never passes through the AI. If neither can reach you, nothing is paid.
  5. Suppliers not on your approved list, hard caps and duplicate orders are blocked, even with approval.
  6. Confirming the same drafts twice never pays twice, and expired drafts cannot be confirmed.

Permissions

  • shop.read: read stock, sales, suppliers and invoices.
  • shop.write: create and confirm purchase-order drafts, pay suppliers within your rules, record deliveries, refunds and rule changes. You can untick this on the consent screen and keep read-only access. Claude will ask you to reconnect if you later ask for a reorder.
  • offline_access: stay connected; the access token lasts 1 hour and is refreshed automatically for up to 30 days of inactivity.

See and revoke connected apps, or delete your account, on the account page.

Limits

  • 120 requests per minute per shop; sign-in is rate limited, and an account locks for 15 minutes after 5 wrong passwords.
  • Lists are short by design: up to 50 low-stock items, and up to 10 top movers and invoices per call.
  • Sales are daily totals; dates are in the shop's timezone (America/New_York for the demo shop).

FAQ

Does ShopVoice Pay read my chats in Claude?

No. It only receives the tool name and arguments Claude sends, such as a product name or a date range. It never reads your conversation, Claude's memory or your files.

Can it pay a supplier?

Yes, within the spending rules you set, through PayPal (sandbox in this demo). Money is held until delivery and charged only for what arrived. Anything over your rules waits for your own approval.

Where is my data stored?

On Render, in the United States (Virginia). See the privacy policy.

Something failed

Every error message says what to try next. If it keeps failing, write to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} (see support).
