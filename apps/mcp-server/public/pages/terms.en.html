The service

ShopVoice Pay is a voice-first purchasing agent for independent grocers. You, and apps you connect such as Claude, can check stock, sales and supplier invoices, draft reorders, and pay suppliers through PayPal, within spending rules you set.

This hosted service is a demo:

  • payments run only in PayPal's sandbox, or in a labelled simulation;
  • no real money moves.

It is free of charge. The source code is open under the MIT licence.

Payments

  • The AI only proposes. ShopVoice Pay's own rules engine decides every payment, and only server code talks to PayPal.

  • ShopVoice Pay is not a bank or a payment service provider. PayPal processes payments under its own terms.
  • Suggested quantities are estimates from sales history, so check them before you order.
